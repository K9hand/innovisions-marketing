Your business has outgrown its tools

The spreadsheets and disconnected apps that worked when you started are now holding you back. Here's what that looks like — and how InnoVisions fixes it.

Your data lives in 5 different places

The reality:

Customers in one app, stock in a spreadsheet, invoices in accounting software, jobs on paper. Nothing talks to each other.

With InnoVisions:

48 deeply integrated modules on one platform. Create a job from a support ticket. Link stock to a job. Generate an invoice from a job card. One entry, everywhere it needs to be.

You don't know if jobs are profitable until it's too late

The reality:

Labour, stock, travel, and on-site costs spread across emails and receipts. You only see the full picture after the job is done — and the margin has disappeared.

With InnoVisions:

Real-time job costing. Every stock item, labour hour, travel log, and on-site order is captured against the job as it happens. Know your margin before you invoice.

Cash flow is always a surprise

The reality:

Invoices sent but not tracked. Bills arrive unexpectedly. You find out about overdue payments when the bank calls — not before.

With InnoVisions:

AR/AP aging dashboards. See who owes you, what you owe, and what's overdue — updated in real time. Cash flow reports, P&L, balance sheet, and VAT summary all one click away.

SARS compliance keeps you up at night

The reality:

PAYE, UIF, VAT — calculated manually or in separate tools. One mistake and you're facing penalties. IRP5 certificates become a December nightmare.

With InnoVisions:

Built-in SARS compliance. PAYE calculations, UIF deductions, IRP5 certificate generation, and VAT summaries — all calculated automatically from your data. Designed for South African tax rules.

Customer follow-ups fall through the cracks

The reality:

Quotes sent but not followed up. Support tickets lost in email threads. SLAs breached because nobody tracked response times.

With InnoVisions:

CRM pipeline tracks every lead and opportunity. Support desk with SLA monitoring. Automated escalations. Technician dispatch with customer notifications. Nothing slips.

You're doing data entry instead of growing the business

The reality:

Customer details typed into three different systems. Stock counted manually. Purchase orders emailed back and forth. Hours wasted on busywork.

With InnoVisions:

One entry, everywhere it's needed. Customer → Quote → Sales Order → Job → Stock Issue → Invoice → Payment → P&L. Every step flows automatically into the next. Your team focuses on work, not data entry.

From customer enquiry to cash in the bank

Every step flows into the next — automatically. No manual handoffs, no duplicate entry, no lost information.

Capture customer & quote

Create a quote from a lead or opportunity. Set line items, pricing, and terms — all linked to the customer profile with full interaction history.

✓ CRM pipeline updates automatically✓ Stock availability checked in real-time

Accept & convert to sales order

Customer accepts via e-signature. The quote becomes a sales order, stock is reserved, and fulfilment workflow begins.

✓ E-signature captured with audit trail✓ Pick list generated for warehouse

Execute job & track costs

If services are involved, a job card is created. Labour hours, materials, travel, and subcontractor costs are captured against the job in real-time.

✓ Labour costs at actual pay rates✓ Material issues deducted from stock

Dispatch & invoice

Goods despatched, job completed. The system generates the invoice automatically from the sales order and job costs — everything flows to Accounts Receivable.

✓ Invoice posts to GL automatically✓ Cost of sales calculated from actual costs

Collect payment & reconcile

Payment is allocated against the invoice. AR aging updates, cash flow reports reflect the receipt, and the bank reconciliation matches the transaction.

✓ Payment allocated automatically✓ Bank feed reconciliation

P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, VAT summary — all updated live. Job profitability, margin analysis, and operational dashboards give you complete visibility.

Built for real businesses

InnoVisions is not a template, a proof of concept, or a weekend project. It's a production-grade ERP platform with over 48 deeply integrated modules.

Why we built it

South African small and medium businesses deserve better software. For too long, the options have been either expensive enterprise platforms designed for multinationals, or disconnected tools that create more work than they save.

We built InnoVisions because we believe that growing businesses should have access to the same quality of tools as large enterprises — without the enterprise price tag or complexity.

Every module is designed to work together. A support ticket can become a job card, which pulls stock from the warehouse, logs technician hours, tracks vehicle travel, and generates a tax invoice — all within one system. No manual handoffs. No duplicate data entry. No lost information.

Our mission

Make enterprise-grade ERP accessible to every growing South African business — without the enterprise price tag, complexity, or lock-in.

Deeply Integrated

Every module talks to every other module. No silos, no manual sync, no duplicate data entry. Information flows naturally from one part of the business to the next.

Built for South Africa

SARS tax tables, ZAR currency, SA address formats, local support. We don't bolt on compliance — it's built into every module from the ground up.

No Lock-In

Self-host or use our cloud. Export your data anytime via CSV, PDF, or API. No proprietary formats, no contracts, no hurdles if you choose to leave. Your data belongs to you.

Fair Pricing

No per-seat fees, no module restrictions, no surprise bills. Pay for the platform, not for each person. A 10-person company and a 100-person company pay the same price.

Built with Care

Production-grade code, comprehensive test coverage, and clean architecture. We don't cut corners because your business data deserves better. Every feature is built to last.

Continuous Improvement

Regular releases with new features, improvements, and compliance updates. SARS tax tables updated annually. Customer feedback drives our roadmap. We're in this for the long term.

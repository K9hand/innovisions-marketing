What you get, regardless of tier

Every plan includes the full platform — no module restrictions, no per-user fees, no hidden costs.

All 48+ modules included

No module restrictions at any tier. Every customer gets the full platform — CRM, jobs, stock, finance, payroll, support, and more. You only pay for hosting and support level.

Unlimited users included

No per-seat pricing. Add your entire operations team, finance team, warehouse staff, technicians, and admin — everyone gets access at no extra cost.

Full REST API included

Every module exposed via OpenAPI-documented REST endpoints. Integrate with your website, accounting tools, banking platforms, or any third-party service.

SARS compliance included

Built-in PAYE tax tables, UIF/SDL calculations, VAT summary, and IRP5 certificate generation. Calculated automatically from your data using current SARS rates and rules.

Immutable audit trail included

Every transaction, every change, every login logged with timestamp and user identity. Full audit trail for financial compliance, POPIA requirements, and operational accountability.

Self-service migration included

Import tools for customers, suppliers, stock items, and chart of accounts. Move from spreadsheets or legacy systems with built-in data import wizards. No professional services fee required to get started.
